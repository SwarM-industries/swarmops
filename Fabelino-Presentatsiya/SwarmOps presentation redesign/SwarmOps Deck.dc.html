ARCHITECTURE

Six services, one gateway

Small pieces instead of one big program. Each can fail and recover on its own.

gateway the only public door, routes every request
auth log-in, tokens and roles
fleet the drone registry: state, battery, payload
mission orders and their lifecycle, creation to completion
planning the brain: assigns drones to missions, replans in flight
telemetry live positions and battery, streamed off the bus
notify pushes alerts to operators the moment they matter
RabbitMQ message bus services talk through queues, never directly
drone simulator virtual drones feeding the bus
