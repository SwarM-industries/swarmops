CONTEXT

Nobody sees the whole airspace

Each drone is locked to one controller No shared picture of who is flying what, who already swept an area, and which information is current.
A coordination problem, not a fleet problem Independent operators in one sector are a swarm with no brain.
Nobody sells the layer above Every tool serves the single operator, not whoever owns the airspace.
15% of flight time is lost to suboptimal coordination (estimate).

THE SOLUTION

One live picture,
coordinated in real time

VRPTW routing engine Optimal routes, automatic mission and battery planning.
Dynamic re-planning The task board updates within seconds of a failure or a priority change.
Unified control room One live map, every aircraft, every camera feed on demand.
Hardware agnostic MAVLink, DJI and Autel in a single console.

The system is built, deployed and running on AWS.
